How It Works

Ordering is easy — no app, no account needed

1

Pick Items

Browse stock and add items to your basket.

2

Checkout asks for your name, phone & location — pick free pickup (Posta / Mwembe Naivas), Friday rider, or a bus parcel.

Payment & Delivery

Simple, safe and transparent

💳 M-Pesa Payment

Buy Goods Till: 1234567 (HAVANNAH KIDS CLOSET)

We only ever ask for payment to this Till number — never to a personal number. Send your M-Pesa confirmation on WhatsApp after paying.

🗓 Delivery Options & Schedule

• FREE pickup (Mombasa): 📮 Posta (Mombasa CBD) or 🏬 Mwembe (Naivas Mall) — arrange day on WhatsApp

• Mombasa rider: every Friday — Island, Likoni, Nyali, Bamburi, Kisauni · KSh 150

• Coast counties parcel: Thursdays twice a month via Tahmeed or Mash Poa only · bus rate + service fee

• Other counties parcel: Thursdays twice a month via Ena Coach, Mash Poa or Tahmeed · bus rate + service fee, paid on collection

💡 Parcel fee = the bus company's current rate for your destination + a flat $0.50 (≈ KSh 65) service fee. The exact amount is calculated automatically at checkout once you pick your county and bus.

🎵 Follow us on TikTok @havannahkidscloset — we announce the exact Thursday parcel dates & Friday routes there every week.

🔒 Your privacy is protected. This site collects no personal data, uses no cookies or trackers, and stores nothing on any server. Your order goes directly from your device to our WhatsApp over an encrypted connection. GitHub Pages serves this site over HTTPS.
